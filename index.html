--- layout: page title: Namescape subtitle: Learning about place through naming --- Hello from the unceded territories of the [lək̓ʷəŋən (Esquimalt and Songhees Nations)](https://www.songheesnation.ca/community/l-k-ng-n-traditional-territory) and [WSÁNEĆ](https://wsanec.com/history-territory/) Peoples**, on the lands known also as [Vancouver Island](https://www.bcafn.ca/first-nations-bc/interactive-map?region=100). **"Namescapes" are the landscapes of place names, or toponyms, we create culturally and individually to name the places around us.** Namescapes are part of our shared knowledge systems and histories. ### More about this site This site reflects part of my journey to understand ways to encourage new harmonies between settler and Indigenous namescapes, especially in and around the Salish Sea. I also want to celebrate a growing chorus of those working toward reconciliation—an evolving and understandably contested term—through the acknowledgment, restoration, and creation of Indigenous toponyms. As part of this journey, [TEMOSEṈ](https://wsanec.com/avada_portfolio/temoseng-chazz-elliott/), [Jesse Campbell](https://www.campbellvisualarts.com/), and I ([Kim Shortreed](https://kimshortreed.github.io/namescape/about/)) collaborated on an art-installation project, the _Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ map, which is intended to get us to think differently about how we learn about the [SENĆOŦEN places and place names](https://itservices.cas.unt.edu/~montler/saanich/wordlist/placenames.html) around us, cartographically and historically. **Click on the image below to see a virtual, interactive version of the _Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ map we created together:** [![screenshot of the virtual version of the map installation](assets/img/virtual-map-screenshot.png)](https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=QNwzN9nmvzg) ### What next... Check out more about the making of and thinking behind the _Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ map (in the UNTITLED ṮEṮÁĆES MAP menu, above). Or check out the NAMESCAPE sections for more about place names and related resources. **For all the educators out there**, be sure to check out the **[_Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ Online Education Package](https://legacy.uvic.ca/gallery/untitledtetaces/)**, designed and hosted with great generosity by UVic's Legacy Galleries. This education package provides some **excellent resources, videos, and exercises for K-12 students, teachers, and anyone else who wants to learn more about naming, place, and territory** through the lens of the _Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ art exhibition.
