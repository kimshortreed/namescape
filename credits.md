The _Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ map, the first haptic map prototype, was built collaboratively. Kim Shortreed came up with the concept for a "haptic map" as part of his PhD project, "[Contracolonial Practices in Salish Sea Namescapes](https://dspace.library.uvic.ca/items/ca4689bc-5f3f-42c3-9f61-e980c2eedf65)." 

Kim brought the idea to TEMOSEṈ Chazz Elliott and they co-created the concept for the _Untitled ṮEṮÁĆES_ map. TEMOSEṈ designed and carved the outside panels with help from Matthew Parlby-Elliott. Kim built the map's structure (with help from TEMOSEṈ, Matt, and Ben Olsen) and the interactive islands. Jesse Campbell painted the seascape horizon.
